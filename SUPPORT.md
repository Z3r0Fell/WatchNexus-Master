# Support

Need help with WatchNexus? Here's where to go.

## Documentation

- Setup & usage: see the project `docs/` directory and the in-app first-run
  wizard (OOBE), which checks FFmpeg and walks you through configuration.

## Questions & discussion

- Open a **GitHub Discussion** for usage questions, ideas, and "how do I…".
- Search existing issues/discussions first — your question may be answered.

## Bug reports

- Open a **GitHub Issue** with: WatchNexus version, OS, reproduction steps,
  expected vs actual behavior, and relevant logs (redact any secrets/tokens).

## Security issues

- **Do not** open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

## Commercial / licensing (Pro / Ultra)

- For license activation or edition questions, contact **support@watchnexus.ca**.
