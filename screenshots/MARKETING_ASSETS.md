## Key Selling Points for Kickstarter

### 🎯 One App to Replace Six
WatchNexus replaces:
- ❌ Sonarr → ✅ Built-in TV management
- ❌ Radarr → ✅ Built-in movie management  
- ❌ Prowlarr → ✅ Compote indexer system
- ❌ qBittorrent → ✅ Syrup torrent engine
- ❌ Bazarr → ✅ Automatic subtitles
- ❌ Jellyfin → ✅ Full media player

### 🔥 Unique Features
1. **Theme Forge (Milk)** - Visual theme editor with 6 presets
2. **Gadgets** - Plugin system that converts Kodi/Jellyfin/Plex plugins
3. **Continue Watching** - Per-user watch progress across devices
4. **Quality Profiles** - Sonarr-like automation built-in
5. **Local/Remote Detection** - Smart login experience
