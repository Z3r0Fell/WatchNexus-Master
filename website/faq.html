What is WatchNexus?

WatchNexus is a self-hosted, all-in-one media pipeline. It replaces Sonarr, Radarr, Prowlarr, qBittorrent, Bazarr, Jellyfin, and more with a single application containing 73 purpose-built modules, each with a culinary codename.

Is there a free version?

Yes. WatchNexus Standard is completely free and includes 31 modules — media library management, streaming, downloads, playlists, gadgets, themes, and more. No credit card, no trial period, no expiry.

What's the difference between Standard, Pro, and Ultra?

Standard (Free) — 31 modules covering core media server functionality: library, streaming, downloads, playlists, and gadgets.

Pro ($35 CAD) — Adds 18 modules: indexer search, movie automation, scheduled tasks, analytics, AI recommendations, Live TV/IPTV, DVR, ebooks, music, RSS, backup, and 2FA.

Ultra ($60 CAD) — Adds 24 more modules: security center, GPU transcoding, disc ripping, gaming console, media sync from Jellyfin/Emby, Jellyseerr, cloud backup, offline sync, Matrix, Usenet, and more.

Is this a subscription or a one-time payment?

One-time payment. Buy a Pro or Ultra license once and it's yours forever. Standard is free forever. All tiers include all future updates.

How do I activate Pro or Ultra?

1. Install WatchNexus Standard (free) via Docker
2. Purchase a Pro or Ultra serial number at licenses.watchnexus.ca
3. In the app, go to Settings → Activation and enter your serial
4. Modules unlock immediately — no restart required

Can I upgrade from Pro to Ultra later?

Yes. Upgrade at any time by purchasing an Ultra serial and entering it in Settings → Activation. The app handles tier transitions automatically.

What are the system requirements?

Docker 20.10+ on Linux, macOS, or Windows (WSL2). Minimum 2 CPU cores and 2 GB RAM. For hardware transcoding (Ultra), an NVIDIA GPU with NVENC, Intel Quick Sync, or AMD AMF is supported.

How do updates work?

WatchNexus has a built-in update system. It checks the license server for new builds and can silently apply hotfix patches. You can configure auto-check intervals and update channels (Stable, Beta, Nightly) in Settings → Updates.

What is the Gaming Console (Pretzel)?

Pretzel is a browser-based retro game emulator built into WatchNexus Ultra. It supports 15 systems including NES, SNES, N64, Genesis, Game Boy, GBA, PlayStation, Atari, and MAME. Load ROMs from your server and play directly in the browser.

What is Chowder (Media Sync)?

Chowder is a JellyLooter Pro-powered engine that connects to remote Jellyfin or Emby servers, browses their libraries, and downloads content with scheduling, bandwidth throttling, and automatic library import. Ultra only.

Is my data stored locally?

Yes — 100% self-hosted. Your media, database, settings, and watch history stay on your server. The only external connections are TMDB (for metadata), your configured download clients, and the license server (for activation and updates only).

What currency are prices listed in?

Canadian Dollars (CAD). Pro is $35 CAD (~$26 USD) and Ultra is $60 CAD (~$44 USD). One-time, no recurring fees, no hidden charges.
