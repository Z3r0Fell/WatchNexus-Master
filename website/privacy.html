Privacy policy

Short version: your data stays on your server.

Last updated: August 2026

Data collection

WatchNexus is a self-hosted media server. All your data stays on your own infrastructure. We do not collect, transmit or store any of your personal data, media files or usage information on our servers.

License server

When you activate a license key, WatchNexus communicates with our license server at licenses.watchnexus.ca to validate your key. This transmission includes only the license key and a hardware identifier. No other data is sent.

Third-party services

WatchNexus may connect to third-party APIs such as TMDB for metadata. These connections are initiated by your instance and are governed by the respective third-party privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at privacy@watchnexus.ca.
