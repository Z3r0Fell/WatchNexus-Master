About WatchNexus

WatchNexus is a self-hosted, unified media management pipeline that combines the best of media servers like Jellyfin with the automation power of the *arr ecosystem -- all in a single application.

Built with C#/.NET 10 and React, it gives users complete control over their media libraries without relying on third-party cloud services. One self-contained binary, 73 modules, zero dependencies.
