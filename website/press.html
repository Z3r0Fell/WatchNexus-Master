About WatchNexus

WatchNexus is a self-hosted media server that combines the playback of Jellyfin-style servers with the automation of the *arr ecosystem — in a single application.

Built with C#/.NET 10 and React, it gives people complete control of their media libraries without relying on third-party cloud services. It’s made in Canada by Nexus Media, alongside the NexusSMS messaging app and the NexusAudio music toolkit.
