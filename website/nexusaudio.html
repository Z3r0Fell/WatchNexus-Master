Downloader

From Spotify to your library.

Your format, your quality, your folder — queued, tagged and lyrics-embedded.

  • Track, album or playlist — paste a Spotify URL and get the full track list.
  • No API key required — the built-in scraper works out of the box; optional keys speed up big playlists.
  • Formats & quality — MP3 (64–320 kbps), FLAC, M4A or OGG.
  • Age-restricted content — pass browser cookies from Firefox, Chrome, Edge, Brave or Safari.
  • Download queue — batch whole albums with progress bars and one-click cancel.
  • Auto-tagging — title, artist and album embedded on every download.
  • Spreadsheet editing — title, artist, album, genre, year and track number across every file at once.
  • Bulk rename — rename every file to “Artist – Title” in one click.
  • Find & replace — across tags, with regex and case toggles.
  • Extended tags — composer, album artist, disc, BPM, ISRC, publisher and more.
  • Cover art — download and embed front covers in MP3, FLAC, M4A or OGG.
  • All four formats — ID3v2, Vorbis Comments, MP4 tags and OGG.

Soulseek P2P

The network for rare tracks.

Soulseek is where niche and hard-to-find music lives. NexusAudio speaks the protocol natively.

  • Native SLSK protocol — direct TCP implementation, no third-party client.
  • Search the network — results with user, size, bitrate and length.
  • Direct downloads — double-click a result to fetch it from the peer.
  • Reliable connections — auto-reconnect with exponential backoff and keepalive.
