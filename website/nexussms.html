Highlights

Built for privacy. Built for power.

The messaging features you expect, plus security and automation you won’t find in a stock messaging app.

End-to-end encryption

AES-256-GCM with Signal-style X3DH + Double Ratchet, 30-digit safety numbers and QR key verification.

Unified inbox

Matrix, Telegram, Discord, Messenger, Signal and Slack as filterable tabs — reply to all from one app.

Spam & scam protection

On-device detection across 10 scam categories — OTP phishing, bank impersonation, delivery scams — with auto-blocking.

Automation rules

A Tasker-lite engine with 15 actions: auto-copy OTPs, auto-reply, archive, forward to email, webhooks and more.

Encrypted backups

Google Drive or your own WebDAV/Nextcloud, protected with PBKDF2 passphrase encryption. One-tap restore.

Deniable vault

A hidden encrypted space with a decoy PIN. Hide conversations and unlock them with your own code.

Messaging

Everyday messaging, done right.

From scheduled texts to smart replies, the basics are anything but basic.

  • SMS / MMS + RCS — text, photos, video and rich messaging with automatic SMS fallback.
  • Scheduled messages — send once, or repeat daily, weekly or monthly.
  • Shortcodes — type !brb and it expands to “Be right back”.
  • Signatures & templates — per-conversation signatures and a template library.
  • Smart reply — context-aware suggestion chips for fast replies.
  • GIFs, stickers & emoji — GIF picker, six sticker packs and searchable emoji.
  • Reactions — long-press any message to react.
  • Full-text search — across every conversation, instantly.
  • Dual SIM — choose which SIM each message goes out on.
  • Groups & bulk SMS — MMS group chats and bulk modes with send-delay protection.
  • App lock — PIN, pattern or password with biometric fallback and auto-lock.
  • E2E encrypted chats — per-contact AES-256-GCM with X3DH and Double Ratchet.
  • Safety numbers — verify with a 30-digit fingerprint or in-person QR scan.
  • Deniable vault — a decoy PIN reveals only an empty shell.
  • Screenshot blocking — optional FLAG_SECURE keeps chats out of screenshots.
  • Secure storage — keys backed by the Android Keystore. No plaintext secrets.
